HOME PAGE

 

Celebrating a Journey Through Art

 

Seva, Sankalp, Samarpan: Life of Narendra Modi is an ambitious national art initiative conceived by Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam Social Welfare Trust.

 

The mission is to bring together artists from across India to create 1,000 original paintings portraying different chapters, moments, ideas and milestones associated with the life and public journey of Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

 

From his formative years in Vadnagar to his years in public life, the collection is envisioned as a visual journey through Seva, Sankalp and Samarpan: Service, Resolve and Dedication. The Prime Minister's official biography describes his beginnings in Vadnagar and his progression through grassroots organizational work, administration in Gujarat and national leadership.

 

Our Grand Vision

 

1,000 Artists. 1,000 Canvases. 1 National Art Movement.

 

The initiative proposes to create one of the largest curated artistic collections dedicated to documenting the life and public journey of Narendra Modi.

 

Rather than creating 1,000 similar portraits, participating artists will be encouraged to interpret different chapters of the story through their individual artistic language.

 

 

 

 

THE 1,000 PAINTINGS MISSION

 

The collection can be curated into major galleries covering:

 

1. Roots & Childhood: Vadnagar, family, education and formative experiences

 

 

2. Youth & Spiritual Journey: travel, exploration, discipline and self-discovery

 

 

3. Life of Service: grassroots organization and public engagement

 

 

4. Gujarat Years: administration, development and governance

 

 

5. Journey to National Leadership: the transition to Prime Minister

 

 

6. Transforming India: infrastructure, financial inclusion, sanitation, housing, healthcare and digital transformation

 

 

7. Atmanirbhar Bharat & Innovation: manufacturing, entrepreneurship, startups, technology and space

 

 

8. India on the Global Stage: diplomacy, G20, international partnerships and the Global South

 

 

9. Culture & Civilizational Heritage: India's traditions, heritage and cultural initiatives

 

 

10. Viksit Bharat 2047: artists' interpretations of India's future

 

 

 

This structure makes the collection a visual biography, rather than simply a portrait exhibition.

 

 

 

 

THE GRAND EXHIBITION

 

Proposed National Exhibition

 

Parliament Complex, New Delhi

 

Subject to permission and approval of the competent authorities

 

The long-term aspiration is to seek permission for a specially curated selection of the paintings to be exhibited within the Parliament Complex in New Delhi, giving Members of Parliament and invited dignitaries an opportunity to experience the artistic collection.

 

Parliament's Press and Public Relations arrangements specifically include granting permission for exhibitions in Parliament precincts in specified circumstances, so a formal proposal and approval process would be required.

 

The wider 1,000-work collection could subsequently travel to important cultural venues across India.

 

 

 

 

ART FOR A CAUSE

 

From Canvas to Compassion

 

Following exhibition, selected artworks are proposed to be offered through a professionally administered charity art auction.

 

The initiative intends that an approved portion of eligible auction proceeds be contributed to the Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF), subject to the final auction structure, applicable laws and required approvals.

 

PMNRF accepts voluntary contributions from individuals and institutions and supports relief following natural calamities, major accidents and certain medical treatments.

 

For credibility, the actual donation should be remitted directly through the official PMNRF mechanism and subsequently disclosed transparently with appropriate documentation.

1,000 ARTISTS OF INDIA

 

Calling India's Artists

 

We envision participation from:

 

Legendary Artists • National Awardees • Senior Artists • Contemporary Artists • Folk & Tribal Artists • Women Artists • Young Artists • Art Institutions • Fine Arts Colleges • Emerging Talent

 

Each selected artist will receive a defined theme from the overall artistic narrative while retaining creative freedom over interpretation and technique.

 

Suggested Canvas

 

Recommended: 4 ft × 5 ft

Medium: Artist's choice

Style: Artist's individual expression

 

Every work should be an original artistic interpretation, creating diversity across the final collection.

 

 

 

 

INTERNATIONAL ACHIEVEMENTS GALLERY

 

A dedicated segment can portray India's international engagement during Modi's premiership through paintings covering themes such as:

 

G20 Presidency • International Solar Alliance • International Day of Yoga • Global South • India-US Relations • India-Russia Relations • India-France Relations • India-Japan Relations • India-Middle East Engagement • India-Africa Partnership • Diaspora Connect • Climate Action • Vaccine Maitri • Space Diplomacy • Cultural Diplomacy

 

This section should distinguish between Narendra Modi's leadership role and achievements involving the Government of India, institutions, scientists, diplomats, armed forces, businesses and citizens.

 

 

 

 

PRESENTATION TO NATIONAL LEADERS

 

A Canvas of Appreciation

 

Subject to their consent and protocol, selected paintings are proposed to be formally presented to senior Ministers, constitutional authorities, distinguished public servants and national institutions.

 

Each presentation can include:

 

Original Painting + Artist Profile + Certificate of Authenticity + Story Behind the Canvas + Exhibition Catalogue

 

This creates a lasting cultural record beyond the exhibition itself.

 

 

 

 

THE DIGITAL MUSEUM

 

The physical collection should have a permanent digital counterpart.

 

Each of the 1,000 paintings can receive its own page containing:

 

Painting No. • Artwork Title • Artist • State • Medium • Dimensions • Story • Historical Context • Artist's Interpretation • Exhibition History • Auction Status

 

A QR code beside every physical painting can open its digital museum page.

 

This could eventually become the most valuable long-term asset of the project.
